# Visual direction

Cap: 500 words. How the look was decided, what was tried, and what it cost. `DESIGN.md` records the result as numbers. This records the reasoning.

## What was decided

The taste, in a paragraph. Starter-kit ships the near white and near black direction Kalinga settled. Say whether this product keeps it or changes it, and what changes. An accent is chosen at setup or here, follows "Adding an accent" in `DESIGN.md`, and its hex values and the reason go in this paragraph.

## What was tried, in order

One line per board or reference: what it was, the model and price if generated, and why it lost or won. The boards live in `explorations/`.

## Where the warmth comes from

Photography, illustration, the mark, or nothing. This is the one question the monochrome direction forces.

The paragraph the `image` skill starts from. The palette, the type, the no borders rule, the device, the states to show.
